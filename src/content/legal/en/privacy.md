This Privacy Policy describes how Bankey LLC, doing business as Shaheen Money ("Shaheen Money," "we," "our," or "us"), collects, uses, shares, stores, and protects personal information when you use the Shaheen Money mobile application, website, APIs, and related services (collectively, the "Services").

Your privacy is important to us. We are committed to protecting your personal information and being transparent about how it is collected and used.

By using the Services, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy.

## 1. Scope

This Privacy Policy applies to all users of the Shaheen Money platform, including:

- Mobile application users
- Website visitors
- Business customers
- Merchant users
- Connector Network participants
- API customers
- Individuals interacting with our customer support

This Privacy Policy does not apply to information collected directly by independent Third-Party Providers, including regulated financial institutions, payment processors, identity verification providers, or other partners operating under their own privacy policies.

## 2. Information We Collect

The information we collect depends on how you use the Services.

### Information You Provide

When creating an account, we may collect:

- Full name
- Email address
- Mobile phone number
- Country of residence
- Preferred language
- Profile photo (optional)

Certain Services may request additional information when required by applicable law or by an independent Third-Party Provider.

### Wallet Information

When you create a wallet, we may associate your account with:

- Public wallet address(es)
- Supported blockchain networks
- Wallet preferences
- Transaction history displayed within the application

Shaheen Money does not collect or store your private keys, secret recovery phrase, or other credentials that provide direct control over your self-custody wallet, except where you voluntarily use an optional recovery feature that expressly requires limited encrypted backup information.

### Device Information

To improve security and reliability, we may automatically collect technical information such as:

- Device type
- Operating system
- App version
- Language settings
- Device identifiers
- IP address
- Time zone
- Crash reports
- Security logs

This information helps us diagnose technical issues, prevent fraud, and improve the Services.

### Usage Information

We may collect information regarding how you interact with the Services, including:

- Features accessed
- Screens viewed
- Session duration
- Transaction requests
- Login activity
- Customer support interactions
- Notification preferences

We use this information to improve user experience and platform performance.

### Location Information

With your permission, we may collect approximate or precise location information to:

- locate nearby Connectors;
- display participating merchants;
- improve fraud detection;
- support regulatory compliance where applicable.

You may disable location permissions through your device settings, although certain features may become unavailable.

## 3. How We Use Information

We use personal information to:

- provide and maintain the Services;
- create and manage user accounts;
- facilitate blockchain transactions;
- connect users with participating merchants and Connectors;
- improve platform performance;
- authenticate users;
- secure user accounts;
- detect and prevent fraud;
- respond to customer support requests;
- communicate service updates;
- comply with applicable legal obligations;
- develop new products and features.

We process only the information reasonably necessary for these purposes.

## 4. Blockchain Information

Blockchain networks are public and decentralized.

Transactions conducted on supported blockchain networks may be permanently recorded on public ledgers that are not controlled by Shaheen Money.

Information visible on public blockchains may include:

- wallet addresses;
- transaction amounts;
- timestamps;
- transaction hashes;
- blockchain metadata.

Shaheen Money cannot modify, delete, or erase information recorded on public blockchain networks.

Users should understand that blockchain transactions may remain publicly accessible indefinitely.

## 5. Third-Party Service Providers

Certain regulated financial services are provided by independent Third-Party Providers.

These services may include:

- identity verification;
- fiat deposits;
- bank transfers;
- debit card funding;
- cash pickup;
- cash withdrawals;
- business onboarding;
- payment processing;
- settlement services;
- compliance screening.

When you choose to use these services, the applicable Third-Party Provider may collect personal information directly from you in accordance with its own privacy policy and legal obligations.

Depending on the service selected, Shaheen Money may share only the information reasonably necessary to facilitate your request, such as:

- your name;
- contact information;
- wallet address;
- transaction details;
- other information required to complete the requested service.

Shaheen Money does not control the privacy practices of independent Third-Party Providers. We encourage you to review their privacy policies before using their services.
