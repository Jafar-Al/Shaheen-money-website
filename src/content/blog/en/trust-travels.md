## Trust Is Currency

In the Arab world, trust is currency. It's the handshake at the market, the promise between neighbors, the family network that never fails you. But as finance became digital, trust became mechanical — automated, distant, cold. Shaheen brings it back.

## The New Trust Nodes

Our Connectors — real people, real faces — are the new trust nodes of the digital age. They turn technology into something human again. Each transaction is more than data — it's a shared act of belief between people who care.

"Trust is no longer stored in institutions — it travels between people."

> We don't just move money. We move trust.
