## A Region of Crossroads

The Middle East and North Africa have always been crossroads of trade, culture, and innovation. But when it comes to digital finance, the region remains divided — fragmented by borders, currencies, and outdated systems.

Shaheen Money was born from a simple idea: the region deserves better. A wallet that belongs to its people — not to a bank, not to a government, not to a single flag.

## The First Borderless Digital Economy for MENA

Shaheen is the first borderless digital economy built for MENA, by MENA. It connects freelancers, families, and small businesses from Casablanca to Cairo, from Beirut to Dubai — empowering them to send, receive, and build across invisible boundaries.

"This is the next chapter of regional unity — not through politics, but through prosperity."

> When borders fade, opportunity rises. Shaheen — Born in MENA. Built for everyone.
