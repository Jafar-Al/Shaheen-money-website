## How these get made

Each film is **live plates + brand elements composited on top.** That split is not a compromise — it is how this kind of commercial is actually made, and here it is also what keeps the films honest.

| Generated by the AI tool (the plate) | Added afterwards (by me) |
|---|---|
| The room, the street, the light, the people | The Shaheen falcon |
| Hands, the phone body, the laptop body | Everything on any screen |
| The sky, the clouds, the shop, the counter | The shop's Connector sign |
| Real banknotes in a hand | The portal / liquid-glass effect |
| | Light bursts, the falcon's wake |
| | Titles and the end lockup |

**Why the screens must stay blank in the plate:** an AI tool asked to render "a banking app" will invent an interface, a balance and a transaction. Every one of those would be a fabricated number on a financial company's advertising — the exact thing the website refuses to do (see `CONTENT-NEEDED.md`). It will also not be your app. So every prompt below says the screen is **off, black, blank**, and I put the real thing on it.

**Why the falcon must not be generated:** it is your mark, traced from your brand file. A generated bird is a different bird in every shot. The falcon I composite is `src/assets/brand/falcon.svg` — the same one on the website.

---

## What makes a plate usable

Read this before generating anything. A beautiful plate that breaks these is unusable and has to be regenerated.

1. **Lock the camera, or move it slowly and in one direction.** A locked-off tripod shot is ideal. A slow push or a single tilt is fine. Handheld wobble, snap zooms and orbits make it impossible to stick an element to the plate by hand.
2. **Screens are OFF.** Black, blank, no glow, no reflection of an interface. A faint grey rectangle is fine; a rendered UI is not.
3. **Nothing crosses in front of the screen** — no thumb over the display, no hair, no steam. Anything that overlaps it has to be masked out frame by frame.
4. **Keep the screen flat to camera-ish.** Up to about 30° off-axis is fine. Extreme angles make the replacement read as a sticker.
5. **No on-screen text anywhere in frame.** AI tools produce garbled lettering on signs, cups and laptops; it will be the first thing a viewer notices.
6. **Leave the shop's sign board empty.** A blank board, a plain awning, or a lit-but-blank box.
7. **Match the light across shots in the same film.** Same time of day, same direction, same warmth.
8. **Hands: one clear action, no fast fingers.** Generated hands fail on complex articulation. Slow, simple, deliberate.

**Specs:** 1920×1080 minimum (2160p if your tool offers it) · 24fps or 30fps, consistent within a film · no music or voice on the plate · deliver as MP4 or MOV, highest quality the tool exports.

**Length:** most tools cap a single generation at 5–10 seconds, so each film below is broken into **two or three short shots**. That is better filmmaking anyway — and it means one bad shot costs one regeneration, not the whole film.

# Film 01 — Out of the screen

**10.0s · the falcon leaves the website and climbs into real sky**

Golden hour. A café terrace. Somebody's laptop is open on the table with shaheen.money on it. The screen brightens, and the falcon comes out of it — and the camera can't do anything but follow it up.

### Shot breakdown

| Time | Shot | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| 0.0 – 3.2 | **1A** — medium, laptop on a café table, slow push in | Establish. Warm late light, life blurred behind. The screen is dark, then I light it with the site. |
| 3.2 – 5.6 | **1B** — tighter on the screen, push continues | The screen brightens. The falcon bursts out toward camera. |
| 5.6 – 10.0 | **1C** — fast tilt up from the terrace to open sky | The camera whips up. The falcon climbs into real cloud and holds against the sky. End card. |

The whip-tilt between 1B and 1C hides the cut. That is deliberate: it is the most forgiving transition in this kind of film and it buys you a clean join between two separate generations.

### Prompts

**Shot 1A — 4 seconds**

```
Cinematic medium shot, an open silver laptop on a weathered wooden café table
on an outdoor terrace, late golden hour, warm low sun raking from the left.
The laptop screen is completely switched off, black and blank, angled about
20 degrees away from camera and clearly readable as a flat rectangle. A
ceramic cup and a folded newspaper beside it. Background: a softly blurred
city terrace, a few out-of-focus people sitting and walking, warm bokeh from
string lights. Camera locked on a tripod with a very slow dolly push forward,
about 10cm over the whole shot. 35mm lens, shallow depth of field, f/2.0.
Anamorphic, subtle film grain, natural colour, no colour cast.

Negative: no text anywhere, no logos, no on-screen interface, no screen glow,
no reflections on the screen, no hands entering frame, no camera shake, no
zoom, no people in focus, no birds.
```

**Shot 1B — 3 seconds**

```
Cinematic close shot pushing in on the dark blank screen of an open laptop on
a café table at golden hour, warm rim light along the top edge of the screen.
The screen stays completely black and empty. Camera pushes in steadily and
slightly low, ending with the screen filling two thirds of frame. 50mm lens,
shallow depth of field. Warm practical light, soft highlight on the aluminium
edge. Subtle film grain.

Negative: no text, no logo, no user interface, no light emitting from the
screen, no hands, no reflection of a person, no shake, no birds, no particles.
```

**Shot 1C — 4 seconds**

```
Cinematic shot that begins on a blurred café terrace and whip-tilts rapidly
upward to reveal wide open sky, late golden hour. Real cumulus clouds lit warm
on their undersides, deep blue above, a few high wisps. The tilt decelerates
and settles, holding on empty sky with clouds drifting slowly. Handheld-free,

photographic, no stylisation.

Negative: no birds, no aircraft, no text, no logos, no lens flare artefacts,
no digital sky, no cartoon clouds, no fast cloud movement, no people.
```

### What I add

Site on the laptop screen · the screen brightening · the falcon and its light burst · the falcon's flight through the sky in 1C · end lockup and the line.

# Film 02 — Through the glass

**10.0s · a hand reaches into the phone and pulls out cash**

The one you asked for. A real hand, a real phone, and the screen behaves like a surface you can put your hand through.

### Shot breakdown

| Time | Shot | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| 0.0 – 2.6 | **2A** — close, a hand holding a phone, screen to camera | Establish. I put the app on the screen. |
| 2.6 – 6.4 | **2B** — same framing, a second hand reaches in | The screen goes liquid. The hand pushes through, to the wrist. |
| 6.4 – 10.0 | **2C** — the hand withdraws holding banknotes | It comes back out with cash and lowers out of frame. End card. |

**This is the hardest of the three.** Generated hands fail on complicated articulation, so every prompt keeps the action slow and singular. Expect to regenerate 2B a few times — that is normal for this shot, not a sign anything is wrong.

If your tool cannot do the reach-through convincingly, there is a fallback that always works and is honestly almost as good: shoot **2B as the hand approaching and stopping just short of the screen**, and I make the screen open into a portal and push the cash out to meet it. Prompt included below.

### Prompts

**Shot 2A — 3 seconds**

```

background of a kitchen table. The hand is steady and relaxed, nails clean,
no jewellery. Camera locked on a tripod, no movement. 85mm macro lens, shallow

Negative: no text, no logo, no user interface, no screen glow, no reflection
```

**Shot 2C — 4 seconds**

```
Cinematic extreme close-up, a person's left hand holds a smartphone upright
with the screen facing camera, black and blank. A second hand withdraws slowly
from the centre of the screen toward camera, fingers closed around a small fan
of folded banknotes, and lowers gently out of the bottom of frame. Slow,
deliberate, continuous motion. Soft natural window light from the left, warm
interior. Camera locked on a tripod, no movement. 85mm macro lens, shallow
depth of field. Natural skin tones, subtle film grain, photographic.

Negative: no text on the banknotes, no recognisable currency, no denomination
numbers, no faces on the notes, no logo, no interface, no screen glow, no
distorted fingers, no extra fingers, no fast motion, no camera shake.
```

> **On the banknotes:** the negative prompt asks for no readable denomination and no recognisable currency deliberately. A close-up of a specific note ties the film to one country and, worse, implies an amount. Plain, slightly out-of-focus folded notes read as "cash" everywhere and claim nothing. If the tool insists on producing a recognisable note, tell me and I will blur or replace them in the composite.

### What I add

The app on the phone screen · the liquid-glass portal and its light · the glow around the wrist where it enters · the confirmation on the screen at the end · end lockup and the line.

## When you have the plates

Drop them anywhere in the project and tell me the paths. For each film I need
the two or three shots as separate files, named so the order is obvious
(`01a.mp4`, `01b.mp4`, `01c.mp4`).

Then tell me, per shot:

- anything that moved in frame that I should know about;
- whether the screen stayed genuinely blank;
- which take you prefer if you generated several.

I will conform them to a common frame rate, cut them to the timings above,
composite the brand elements, grade them to match each other, and deliver
three 10-second MP4s.

`motion/lib/` already holds what that needs: the falcon from your brand file,
the real geography, the brand type and colour, and the frame-accurate
renderer. The brand elements get rendered with alpha and composited over your
footage with ffmpeg.
